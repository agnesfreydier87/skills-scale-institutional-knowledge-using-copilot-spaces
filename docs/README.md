# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a structured delivery lifecycle that moves from project initiation to planning, execution, release, and retrospective improvement. Teams begin by confirming the business problem, success metrics, stakeholders, and initial risks, then translate approved work into prioritized backlog items with clear acceptance criteria and milestones.

Delivery is organized around clear cross-functional ownership. Project Managers coordinate plans, dependencies, timelines, and stakeholder communication, while Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize scope. Developers and QA/testing contributors collaborate to build and validate increments, with sponsors and stakeholders supporting decisions and escalation when needed.

Communication and risk management are handled through recurring team cadences and explicit escalation paths. Standups, PM/PdM syncs, demos, and stakeholder updates keep progress visible, while a risk register tracks impact, likelihood, owners, mitigations, and status. Blockers are escalated from the delivery team to project and product leadership, then to sponsors for time-sensitive or high-impact decisions.

Quality is built into everyday execution and release readiness. Teams use small reviewable pull requests, acceptance criteria checks, and CI validation (including tests, linting, and security checks) before merge and deployment. Releases include staging validation, release notes, and rollback planning, followed by retrospectives that convert lessons learned into owned improvement actions.
